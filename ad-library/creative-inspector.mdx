Click any creative — on Home, in a report, on the leaderboard or in a theme — and the **creative inspector** opens. It brings together everything about that one creative, across every ad, ad set, campaign and ad account that runs it.

## The header

The top of the inspector shows the creative's name, its platform and format, its status and its launch date. When the same creative runs in several ads, a **+N** badge shows how many. Switch between **Ad details** and **Analysis** in the middle, change the date range on the right, and use the link button to copy a link to the creative.

On the left, the preview shows the creative as it appears in a placement. Pick the placement (for example **Facebook feed**) and which of its ads to preview, and use the download button to download the image or video.

---

## Ad details

The **Ad details** view has a tab for each part of the creative:

| Tab | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Status, launch date, spend, ROAS and your key metrics; for videos, the **audience retention** curve with its drop-offs; the **placement breakdown** and the **gender & age breakdown**; the creative's AI tags, custom tags and the values read from its name. |
| **Copy** | The primary text, headline and description the ads use. |
| **Ads** | Every ad that runs the creative, biggest spender first, with its campaign and ad set. Pause or start an ad from here. |
| **Performance** | The creative's metrics over the inspector's date range. |
| **Ad comments** | Comments people left on the ads. |
| **Transcript** | For videos: what's said, with timestamps. **Copy transcript** copies it. |
| **Notes** | What your team learned from this creative. |

<Note>
  Pausing or starting an ad from the **Ads** tab changes it on the ad platform. The Ad Library shows the new status after its next sync.
</Note>

## AI analysis

Switch the inspector to **Analysis** to see why an ad works and what to fix. Choose how deep to go, then click **Analyse**:

| Option | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Full analysis** (recommended) | Whether to scale, refine or rebuild the ad, with the fixes ranked; a review of the copy, claims and landing page; and new hooks, angles and tests to run. |
| **Partial analysis** | How the ad is built and who it speaks to: the hook, story and format, scored; the concept underneath it; who it reaches and whether they want it. |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ad-library-analysis-chooser.png" alt="The Analysis view before a run, with the Full analysis and Partial analysis options and the credits left this month" />
</Frame>

The analysis reads the ad the way a creative strategist would. The **Summary** opens with a verdict — **Scale**, **Refine** or **Rebuild** — and the reason, then what to **fix first**, ranked by impact, and **Scores** out of 10 for the hook, story, concept, audience and copy. Tabs along the top go deeper into each part, the **Landing page** and the **Next tests** to run. For videos, a fix links to the moment in the video it's about.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ad-library-analysis.png" alt="A finished Full analysis with a Scale verdict, the fix to make first and scores for hook, story, concept, audience and copy" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  An analysis uses AI credits from your workspace. The chooser shows the most each option can use and how many credits are left this month, and a video uses more than an image. When the analysis is done, the creative's AI tags are filled in too.
</Note>

## Tags on a creative

The **Overview** tab lists the creative's tags:

- **AI tags** — such as hook, angle, persona, format and style. If a tag is wrong, edit it; your edit is marked **Edited** and kept.
- **Custom tags** — your own labels. Click **Add tag** to add one.
- **Naming convention** — values read from the ad's name by your naming rules. **Edit rules** opens them.

See [Tags and settings](/ad-library/tags-and-settings).

## Notes

Open the **Notes** tab and write what you learned from the creative — what worked, what to try next. Press **Enter** to add the note, or **Shift+Enter** for a new line. Every note also appears on the Ad Library's **Notes** page, so the whole team can read them in one place. See [Leaderboard, themes and notes](/ad-library/leaderboard-themes-notes#notes).

## Ask Jade

The inspector suggests **Thought starters** — questions about this creative you can send to [Jade](/jade/overview) in one click, for example what to test next or how to brief a new version.
