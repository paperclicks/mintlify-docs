If the default metrics don't cover something you track, you can define up to **10 custom metrics** using your own formulas. Once created, they appear as columns in any column preset and are also available as conditions inside your automation rules.

---

## Creating a Custom Metric

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Column Management">
    Click the column settings icon at the bottom-left of the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Custom Metric">
    Click **Custom Metric** in the left panel. You will see a list of 10 custom metric slots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit a slot">
    Click the edit icon next to any empty slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the metric">
    Enter a **Column Name**, select the **Format** (Currency or Percentage), and set the **Precision** (number of decimal places to display).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the formula">
    Use the formula builder to select metrics from the dropdown and combine them using `+`, `−`, `×`, `/`, `(`, `)` operators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Your custom metric is immediately available as a column in any preset and as a condition in your automation rules.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Custom metrics are available across all ad networks — not just the one you created them on. Make sure your formula references metrics that exist for each network you plan to use the column with.
</Note>
