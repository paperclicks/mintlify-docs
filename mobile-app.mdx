TheOptimizer has an official mobile app for **iOS** and **Android**. It isn't a read-only companion: you can check performance, make changes to live campaigns, ask Jade for help and fix integration problems without opening a laptop.

## Get the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download it">
    Search for **TheOptimizer** in the **App Store** (iPhone) or **Google Play** (Android) and install the official app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your existing account">
    Use the same login you use on the web. Your ad accounts, trackers, rules, reports and dashboards are already there — there's nothing to set up twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow notifications">
    When the app asks, allow notifications so it can alert you about issues affecting your campaigns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## What you can do from your phone

| Area | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Jade** | Ask your AI copilot to build a report, spot anomalies, highlight what matters, or take action — every change still needs your approval. See [Jade](/jade/overview). |
| **Campaigns** | Start and stop campaigns, adjust bids and budgets, and duplicate campaigns. |
| **Reporting** | Build reports and share them with a link. See [Reporting](/reports/overview). |
| **Dashboards** | Open your boards, build new ones or customize existing ones. See [Dashboard](/dashboard/overview). |
| **Notifications** | Get notified on your phone about anything important affecting your campaigns. See [Notification Settings](/account-settings/notifications). |
| **Integrations** | Connect new ad accounts and fix ad account issues. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview). |
| **Team** | Invite team members to your account. See [Team Members](/account-settings/team-members). |

## A typical on-the-go check

1. A notification tells you an ad account stopped syncing or a campaign is burning budget.
2. Open the app and ask Jade what changed — *"Why did ROI drop on Facebook today?"*
3. Pause the campaign, lower a bid or cut the budget right there.
4. If the cause is an integration problem, reconnect or fix the ad account from **Integrations**.

<Tip>
  Pair the app's notifications with [Notification Settings](/account-settings/notifications) on the web: send noisy alerts like rule executions to email or Slack, and keep your phone for the problems that need you right now.
</Tip>

## Mobile app vs. the web on your phone

The web platform also has [mobile layouts](/finding-your-way#on-your-phone) for when you open TheOptimizer in your phone's browser. The app adds push notifications and a faster, phone-first experience. Setup-heavy work — like building complex rules or launching campaigns in bulk — is still best done on a larger screen.
