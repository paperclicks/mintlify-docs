Your **Account** page holds everything personal to you: your name and email, how you sign in, and the metrics you want front and center. Open it from the profile menu in the top-right corner and choose **Profile**.

{/* TODO: screenshot — the Account page showing the header card, Personal information, Password, and Sign-in methods sections */}

Each person on the team has their own Account page. Nothing you change here affects your teammates.

## Personal information

The top of the page shows your name, your email, and a **Verified** badge once your email address is confirmed.

Below that, **Personal information** holds your **First name** and **Last name** — how you appear across the app. Click **Save changes** to apply; the button activates only once something has actually changed.

Your **Email** is shown here too, but it is read-only. Changing the address you sign in with isn't supported yet — contact support if you need it changed.

<Note>
  If your subscription predates TheOptimizer's current billing system, your profile is managed in the aMember portal instead. In that case this section shows your sign-in **Identifier** as read-only, with a link to update your details in aMember.
</Note>

## Change your password

The **Password** section shows **Your password is set** with a **Change password** button. Click it to reveal three fields:

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Current password** | Your existing password, to confirm it's you |
| **New password** | Between 8 and 128 characters |
| **Confirm password** | Must match the new password |

Click **Update password** to save.

<Note>
  This section only appears if your account has a password. If you signed up with Google and never set one, there is nothing to change here — you sign in through Google instead.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Changing your password does not sign you out of your other devices. If that is what you want, go to [Security](/account-settings/security) and end your other sessions after updating the password.
</Tip>

## Sign-in methods

**Sign-in methods** lists every way you can get into your account. Connecting more than one means a problem with any single method never locks you out.

| Method | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Email and password. Shows as connected once you have set a password |
| **Google** | Sign in with your Google account. Click **Connect** to link it, **Unlink** to remove it |
| **aMember** | Present on legacy accounts managed through the membership portal |

{/* TODO: screenshot — Sign-in methods section showing Email connected and Google with a Connect button */}

<Warning>
  Google can only be unlinked while you have another way in — an email password or an aMember identity. If Google is your only sign-in method, set a password first, then unlink.
</Warning>

## Your KPIs

**Your KPIs** picks up to six metrics that TheOptimizer shows front and center across your dashboards. Drag them to set the order they appear in, then click **Save KPIs**.

{/* TODO: screenshot — Your KPIs section with the metric picker */}

This is a personal setting, so each team member can pick the metrics that matter to their own work — a media buyer watching ROI and EPC, a creative lead watching CTR and impressions.

<Tip>
  Pick fewer than six. The value of the KPI row is that your most important numbers are visible without scanning, and six competing tiles undercuts that.
</Tip>

## Security and sessions

Two-factor authentication and the list of devices you are signed in on live on their own page. The **Security** section links straight to it — see [Security](/account-settings/security).
