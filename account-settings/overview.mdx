Everything that belongs to your account rather than to a campaign lives behind the profile menu in the top-right corner of the app. Click your avatar to open it.

{/* TODO: screenshot — profile dropdown in the top-right header showing Profile / Settings / Billing */}

| Menu item | What you manage there |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Your name and email, password, sign-in methods, two-factor authentication, active sessions, and your KPI metrics |
| **Settings** | Where alerts are delivered (email, Slack, Telegram) and who is on your team |
| **Billing** | Your plan, covered ad spend, payment methods, invoices, and cancellation |

## Who can see what

TheOptimizer distinguishes between the **main account holder** — the person who signed up and owns the subscription — and **team members** invited into that account.

- **Billing** is visible to the main account holder only. Team members do not see the menu item at all.
- **Team** settings are available to the main account holder and to team members with the **Admin** role. Other team members see them only on plans that include multi-user access.
- **Profile** and **Notifications** are available to everyone. Each person manages their own.

<Note>
  Inviting team members requires a plan that includes multi-user access. If your plan is single-user, opening the **Team** tab shows an upgrade prompt instead of the team list.
</Note>
