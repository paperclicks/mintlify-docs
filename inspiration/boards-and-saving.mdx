Boards are where your team keeps the ads worth remembering — a swipe file per product, angle or client. Everyone in your workspace sees the same boards.

---

## Saving an ad

Click **Save** on any ad card, or in the ad's details, and pick a board — or type a name to create a **New board** on the spot. Saved ads show up on the board and in **Discover → Saved**.

To take an ad off a board, click **Unsave** on its card, or open the board, click **Select**, pick the ads and choose **Remove from board**.

---

## Organizing boards

Your boards are listed under **Boards** in the Inspiration sidebar.

- Click **New board** to create one, or put boards in folders to group them.
- A board's menu lets you **Edit board**, **Duplicate board**, **Share board** or **Delete board**.
- **More ideas** on a board suggests live ads in the same visual formats and industries that aren't on the board yet. Click **Add to board** on the ones you like.

## Sharing a board

Click **Share** on a board to choose who can open it:

- **Workspace** — only people in your workspace.
- **Public link** — anyone with the link can view the board. **Reset link** turns the old link off and gives you a new one.

### Publishing to the community

On the **Publish** tab, you can list a board on **Explore community boards**, where other workspaces can find and follow it. Choose a display name and categories, then click **Publish**. Notes and comments stay private. Only workspace admins can publish a board.

To browse what others have published, open **Community** in the Boards section of the sidebar. Boards you follow stay in your sidebar.

Every board your workspace has shared or published is listed in **Settings → Sharing**, where you can turn off a link or unpublish a board.

## Save from the Meta Ad Library with the Chrome extension

The **Save to Inspo** extension for Chrome adds a **Save** button to every ad in the [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/), so you can save ads to your boards without leaving the page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    In Inspiration, go to **Settings → Browser extension** and click **Add to Chrome**, or find **Save to Inspo** on the Chrome Web Store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your browser">
    Back in **Settings → Browser extension**, click **Connect a browser**. If the extension doesn't pick up the connection, copy the token the page shows and paste it into the extension's popup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save ads">
    Open the Meta Ad Library, pick a board in the extension's popup, and click **Save** on any ad. The arrow next to **Save** lets you pick a different board for one ad, and **Save all** saves every ad loaded on the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An ad Inspiration already has is saved at once. An ad it doesn't have yet is **queued**: Inspiration collects the advertiser's ads, and the ad lands on your board when it's ready.

<Note>
  A connection lasts 7 days, so reconnect weekly. The extension saves as you, to your workspace's boards. Disconnect browsers you no longer use in **Settings → Browser extension** — a connected browser keeps saving until it expires or is disconnected, even if its owner leaves the workspace.
</Note>

## Save from Instagram by DM

Send a post, reel or ad to Inspiration in an Instagram DM, and it lands on your boards.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Link your Instagram account">
    Go to **Settings → Instagram** and click **Link an account**. From the Instagram account you want to link, send the code shown on the page as a DM to the Inspiration account named there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share posts in a DM">
    From Instagram, share any post, reel or ad with that account in a DM. It's saved to your boards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each link lasts 30 days. Your team can link several Instagram accounts, up to the limit the page shows.
