The Google Ads Campaign Creator lets you build and launch Google Ads campaigns at scale without opening Google Ads. You set up a campaign, its ad groups and its ads once, add the things you want to test as [groups](/campaign-creator/groups-and-variations), and TheOptimizer builds every combination for you.

<Note>
  The Google Ads Campaign Creator is in **Beta**. Before you start, [connect your Google Ads account](/ad-networks/google-ads/integration/connect).
</Note>

## Supported campaign types

| Campaign type | What it runs |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** | Text ads on Google Search results, matched to the keywords people search for. Includes **AI Max** and built-in **keyword ideas**. |
| **Performance Max** | One campaign across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, built from asset groups. |
| **Display** | Responsive image ads across millions of websites and apps. |
| **Demand Gen** | Image, video and carousel ads on YouTube, Discover and Gmail. |
| **Shopping** | Product ads from your **Merchant Center** feed. |
| **App** | Installs and engagement for your app. Choose **Google Play**, **Apple App Store** or **Both** — Both creates two campaigns, one for Android and one for iOS, with the same settings and ads. |

## Opening the Campaign Creator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the launcher">
    In the left navigation, go to **Campaigns → Create**, then click the **Google Ads** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the ad account">
    Select the Google Ads account to launch in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build, review and publish">
    The creator walks you through three steps: **Create Campaign**, **Review Variations** and **Add Automation & Publish**. See [Build a Google Ads campaign](/ad-networks/google-ads/campaign-uploader/build-a-campaign).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set a level to **Paused** if you want to review the campaigns in Google Ads before they start spending. **Enabled** starts serving as soon as Google approves it.
</Tip>

## Google's check before you publish

When you publish, TheOptimizer first sends every campaign to Google to check, without creating anything. If Google flags something — a headline that's too long, a disallowed claim, a missing setting — the levels with errors are marked in the tree on the left, each issue with its message and how many places it occurs. Click **Fix it** to jump to the field, correct it, and click **Check again**.

If Google's check can't run, you can still publish: the [Campaign Creation Queue](/campaign-creator/overview) then reports anything Google refuses.

## Templates, saved work and existing campaigns

- **Your work is saved as you go.** Close the creator and come back later: it asks whether to **Continue** where you left off or **Start New**.
- **Templates** save a whole setup — every level and its groups — so the team can reuse it. Use **Apply template** to start from one. Shared budgets, lists, audiences and goals only carry over when the template is used in the same ad account.
- **Load from an existing campaign** starts from a campaign already in the ad account, to edit or duplicate it.
- **Naming templates** build each campaign, ad group and asset group name from parts, so large launches stay readable. See [Naming templates](/campaign-creator/naming-templates).
