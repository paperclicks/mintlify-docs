The Snapchat Campaign Creator builds campaigns, ad squads and ads on Snapchat without opening Snapchat Ads Manager. Add what you want to test as [groups](/campaign-creator/groups-and-variations), and every combination is built for you.

<Note>
  The Snapchat Campaign Creator is in **Beta**. Before you start, [connect your Snapchat account](/ad-networks/snapchat/integration/connect).
</Note>

## Opening the creator

In the left navigation, go to **Campaigns → Create** and click the **Snapchat** card. Pick the ad account, then work through three steps: **Create Campaign**, **Review Variations** and **Add Automation & Publish**.

The tree on the left shows the campaigns, ad squads and ads you're building. Every level has a status: **Active** starts delivering as soon as it's created; **Paused** creates it switched off, to turn on in Snapchat later.

---

## Campaign

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign name** | Type it, or build it from parts with a [naming template](/campaign-creator/naming-templates): text, the date, the objective, budget or variants. |
| **Objective** | **Awareness & engagement**, **Traffic**, **Sales**, **App promotion** or **Leads**. It decides the ad squads' optimization goals. |
| **Spend caps** | Optional **daily** and **lifetime** spend caps. The ad squads' budgets spend either way; Snapchat stops the campaign at whichever cap it reaches first. |
| **Schedule** | Start and end dates. Empty start: the campaign starts as soon as it's created. Empty end: it runs with no end. |

To add new ad squads and ads to a campaign you already run, use **Add to an existing campaign**. The campaign itself stays as it is.

---

## Ad squad

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Optimization goal** | The result Snap delivers for, depending on the objective: impressions, swipe ups, video views, 15-second video views, Story opens, landing page views, Pixel purchases, add to cart, page views or sign-ups, and app installs, purchases, sign-ups or add to cart. |
| **Snap Pixel** / **Snap App** | The pixel or app that measures the goal. |
| **Targeting** | Countries and regions; age (13 to 50+) and gender; devices (iOS, Android); languages; interests. |
| **Placement** | **Automatic placements** (recommended), or **Choose placements**: between friends' Stories, or between Publisher, Creator and Spotlight content. |
| **Bid strategy** | **Auto-bid** (Snap bids to get the most results for the budget), **Max bid** or **Target cost**. |
| **Budget** | **Daily** or **Lifetime**. |
| **Schedule** | Optional start and end, within the campaign's dates. |

## Ad

| Format | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Single image or video** | A top snap that opens your website. |
| **App install** | A top snap that opens your app's store page. Add the app name, icon, and the App Store ID, the Google Play package, or both. |
| **Story** | A tile in Discover that opens a series of snaps: a cover image and cover headline, then 1 to 20 tiles. |

Every ad needs a **Snapchat profile** — the Public Profile the ad shows, which Snapchat requires — plus a **brand name**, a **headline** and a **call to action**. For web ads, add the **website URL** and optional **URL parameters**; **Use the tracker's id macros** fills them in for you.

### Media that fits Snapchat

Pick the top snap from your [Media Library](/creative-library/guide). Snapchat wants vertical 9:16 media (1080×1920), images under 5 MB and videos of 3 to 180 seconds. When a picture or video doesn't fit, the creator offers a one-click fix:

- **Crop to 9:16** — crops the image or video in the Media Library editor and saves the vertical version to your Media Library.
- **Compress to 5 MB** — shrinks a heavy image to fit Snapchat's limit.

## Groups that create variations

| Level | Groups |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign** | Budget, Schedule |
| **Ad squad** | Targeting, Placement, Bid, Budget |
| **Ad** | Creative, Destination |

Every combination becomes its own campaign, ad squad or ad. Check them all on **Review Variations** before you publish.

---

## Publish

On **Add Automation & Publish**, attach [rules](/automation/rules-guide) to the new campaigns and publish. The campaigns go to the Campaign Creation Queue, which shows their progress.

<Tip>
  Your work is saved as you go — reopen the creator and choose **Continue** to pick up where you left off. Save a setup as a template and start the next launch with **Apply template**.
</Tip>
