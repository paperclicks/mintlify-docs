TheOptimizer is fully integrated with Snapchat Ads. You can report on, automate and launch Snapchat campaigns from the same place you manage every other network.

To get started, [connect your Snapchat account](/ad-networks/snapchat/integration/connect). See [Ad Networks](/integrations/ad-networks) for how ad network connections and profiles work.

## How Snapchat levels map in TheOptimizer

| Snapchat | TheOptimizer |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign | Campaign |
| Ad Squad | Ad set (ad group) level |
| Ad | Ad |

Rules, reports and bulk actions that work on the ad set level apply to Snapchat **ad squads**.

### Actions you can take

| Level | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign** | Pause or start, change the daily or lifetime spend cap, rename, tag, archive, clone (to another ad account too, with its rules) |
| **Ad squad** | Pause or start, change the daily or lifetime budget, the bid and the bid strategy, rename, clone |
| **Ad** | Pause or start, change the URL, rename, tag, archive, clone |

<Note>
  Bid actions skip ad squads on **Auto-bid**, since Snapchat sets their bids itself.
</Note>
