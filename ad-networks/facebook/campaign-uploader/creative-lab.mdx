The **Creative Lab** builds a creative test from one ad in the Facebook Campaign Creator. It writes and draws variants with AI, learns from your account's winning ads and your landing page, and turns the variants you pick into ads and [groups](/ad-networks/facebook/campaign-uploader/variations) — in one step.

## Opening the Creative Lab

In the Facebook Campaign Creator, on the ad level, click **Creative Lab** at the top right of the **Ad creative** card. Every AI button next to a field (primary text, headline, description, image) opens the Lab on the matching tab too.

The Lab has three columns:

1. **Base** on the left — the ad you're testing from: its primary text, image, and what the Lab learned from your **Winners · last 30 days** (your account's best ads with at least $10 spent) and **From the landing page** (the product, offer, audience and benefits it read from your final URL).
2. **Directions** in the middle — the playbooks to apply, details about what the copy must say or avoid, optional **References**, and how many variants to make.
3. **Results** on the right — the variants, where you pick the ones worth testing.

## What you can generate

| Tab | What it makes |
| --- | --- |
| **Hooks** | New primary texts. |
| **Headlines** | New headlines. |
| **Descriptions** | New descriptions. |
| **Angles** | Whole ads around a different angle — benefit-led, pain point, social proof, urgency, curiosity, story, offer-first or contrarian. |
| **Images** | New images from the base image or from references, in the shape you choose. |
| **Languages** | Translations of the ad for other markets. The languages of the countries your ad sets target are suggested first. |
| **Placement sizes** | Versions of the image for other placements, saved on the base image. Shown for single image ads once the group has an image. |

Set how many variants you want with **−** and **+**, then click **Generate** (for example **Generate 10 hooks**). Generate again for another round; each round is kept, so you can compare them.

## Playbooks

A **playbook** is a saved instruction the AI follows every time you pick it — like a brief to a copywriter. The Lab comes with built-in playbooks, such as **Like my winners**, **Stronger hook**, **Question hook**, **Short & punchy**, **Use numbers**, **Urgency**, **Curiosity gap**, **Social proof**, **Native advertorial**, **UGC selfie**, **Bold text overlay**, **Before / after** and **Problem close-up**.

To make your own, click **New** under **My playbooks**, or **Save as playbook** on a direction that worked. Write the instruction, choose whether it's for **Copy**, **Images** or **Both**, and insert variables the Lab fills in for you: the product, the offer, the audience (from the landing page) and the countries your ad sets target.

---

## Turn picks into a test

Pick the variants worth testing on the right — each pick becomes an ad. Then choose how to group them:

- **One per group** — each variant gets its own group, so every variant is tested on its own.
- **Up to 5 per group, Meta rotates** (text) or **All in one group** (images) — the variants share a group.

Click **Create test** to build the groups and ads, or **Add to this group** to add the picks to the group you're in. On **Placement sizes**, click **Apply sizes** to save the sizes you kept on the image.

<Note>
  Every round of generation spends AI credits from the same balance as Jade. The Lab shows your remaining credits at the top.
</Note>
