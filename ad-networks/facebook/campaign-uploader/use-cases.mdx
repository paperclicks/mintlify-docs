### Add new creatives as new ad sets into an existing campaign

Feed a running test campaign fresh creatives without building a new campaign every time. Upload a batch and the uploader turns each creative into its own ad set (or groups several creatives per ad set with a single option) inside the campaign you already have live. The first launch builds the structure in a few minutes; save it as a template and every future batch becomes upload-and-publish in under a minute — the template already knows how many ad sets to create and where to publish them.

### Upload localized ad sets — one per state or market — into an existing campaign

The advanced, multi-market version of the above. Feed a live campaign a fresh batch of creatives where each state (or country/region) gets its own ad set with its own copy, its own landing page URL, and its own per-placement image crops. Create one group per market on the audience, creative, and destination sections, then match by group-name pattern so every market only pairs with its own copy and its own URL — no crossed markets. Upload each creative's crops once and let the uploader auto-match them to placements by filename, turn off Advantage+ Creative Enhancement for every ad in one click, and keep your exact naming with dynamic naming templates. Save the whole structure as a template and every future batch — new images, same 10 markets — becomes upload-and-publish in about a minute.

### Test the same creatives across multiple audiences and budgets

For example, three audiences × three budgets = nine ad set variations, all generated from a single base ad set.

### Launch the same campaign structure across multiple ad accounts at once

Build your campaign once, then replicate it into as many ad accounts as you want and publish to all of them in a single launch. Diversify delivery, take advantage of separate learning phases per account, or reduce Facebook's initial randomness. Build the structure in your first ad account, duplicate the campaign, switch the ad account on each copy, and publish. If a page or pixel differs per account, adjust those assets on the duplicated campaign so the launch goes through. Save it all as a template and every future multi-account launch becomes upload-and-publish in minutes — one campaign with 30 ad sets across three accounts publishes as 3 campaigns, 90 ad sets, and 90 ads at once.

### Auto-match image crops to placements by filename

Prepare a square, vertical, and landscape version of each creative, include the aspect ratio in the filename (`1x1`, `9x16`, `1.91x1`), and the uploader assigns each crop to its matching placement automatically. Instead of editing placements ad by ad in Meta, every crop is placed across every ad in one step — even when you are launching hundreds of ads.

## When to Use It

The Mass Campaign Uploader is the right tool when:

- You are launching **more than 5–10 campaigns** at once and doing it manually in Ads Manager would take hours
- You want **budget isolation per creative** — one campaign per creative so Facebook's algorithm cannot favour one creative over others
- You need to **test many variables simultaneously** — multiple audiences, budgets, placements, or creative sets — and want all permutations generated automatically
- You are **reusing a proven structure** — same campaign setup, new creatives or new accounts
- You want **automation rules active from the first impression**, not set up hours after launch

For one-off campaigns with a single ad set and a few ads, Facebook Ads Manager is equally fast. The Uploader's value scales with volume and complexity.
